Purpose

Control evidence before it becomes operational truth

Consequential workflows can fail when evidence, authority, provenance or state becomes unreliable. HITL Kernel v3 is designed to stop unsupported certainty or uncontrolled state transitions from silently becoming accepted truth. It does not guarantee correct decisions or prevent all human error.
