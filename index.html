What it is for

Keep evidence, authority and system state from collapsing into one unsupported answer

Consequential workflows can fail when evidence, permission, provenance or state becomes unreliable. HITL Kernel v3 is designed to preserve those distinctions and surface unresolved control failures rather than silently smoothing them over. It does not guarantee correct decisions, replace accountable human judgment or prevent all human error.

Evidence ControlEvidence state stays explicit rather than being converted into unsupported certainty.
Authority SeparationTechnical capability is not treated as organisational permission.
Explicit HOLD StatesRequired conditions that are not established remain visible.
Reconstruction DisciplineClaims stay bounded to the evidence actually executed or inspected.
